# Style index

Film looks grouped by what the picture looks like. Every style makes a 9:16 reel or a 16:9
YouTube video: open its `STYLE.md` for both formats. Name a style by its name or its folder.

## Edit & documentary

| Style | Folder | What it looks like | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1920s Newsreel | [`newsreel`](01-edit-documentary/newsreel/STYLE.md) | A sound newsreel of the late 1920s, cut from real archive material: a searchlight-and-globe ident with a brass fanfare, a newspaper that spins into the lens, a fast and cheerful announcer on an optical soundtrack, place-and-date cards, maps, magazine pages and documents, wipes, flips and irises, and a hot band that can stop dead for the punchline. | History stories, fads and eras, anniversaries, "on this day" reels, brand origin stories, retro launch ads. |
| Broadcast News Package | [`news-package`](01-edit-documentary/news-package/STYLE.md) | A television newsroom's on-air package, built entirely in code: a sting that becomes the channel bug, a rundown of today's stories that opens and closes the bulletin, a lower-third strap that never leaves the bottom of the frame, and full-frame graphics (split-flap price boards, a line breaking a threshold, explainer maps, documents flying out of a city, quote cards, spec cards with proportion bars, stamps) that carry the facts. | News round-ups, weekly bulletins, market updates, launch and earnings recaps, internal company news, election and sports results. |
| Caption News | [`caption-news`](01-edit-documentary/caption-news/STYLE.md) | Social-video news that reads with the sound off. | AI and tech news, a stat that surprises, "the report actually says…" pieces, weekly round-ups, sound-off ads. |
| Constructivist Montage | [`kino-montage`](01-edit-documentary/kino-montage/STYLE.md) | Real archive footage, photographs and documents, printed as one ink-and-paper poster with a single red, and cut on a machine beat. | Industry and company histories, milestones and anniversaries, infrastructure openings, collective-effort stories, "how many people did it take" explainers, archive-led brand films. |
| Live Countdown Split-screen | [`countdown`](01-edit-documentary/countdown/STYLE.md) | An edit style, not a look. | Launches, product drops, election nights, verdicts, cup finals, New Year countdowns. |
| Sports Telestrator | [`telestrator`](01-edit-documentary/telestrator/STYLE.md) | The TV football analysis segment: play the clip at full speed, rewind, freeze it, and draw on the broadcast picture — rings under players' feet, marker arrows for the ball, a red patch for the space nobody covered — then play on, switch angles, count the frames, and flatten the pitch into a board. | Sports analysis, match recaps, coaching breakdowns, racing and esports replays, "what went wrong" explainers of any filmed play. |
| Time-lapse Symphony | [`city-symphony`](01-edit-documentary/city-symphony/STYLE.md) | A city video cut like a piece of minimalist music: time-lapse turns traffic, crowds and clouds into rivers of light, the cuts get denser with the score's subdivisions, one thing is slowed almost to a stop, and the video ends on a hard cut to black on a downbeat. | City and place films, music montages, openers and sizzles, a day at a festival or a factory, tourism and real-estate launches. |
| Visual Investigation | [`visual-investigation`](01-edit-documentary/visual-investigation/STYLE.md) | A cold investigation desk: footage, photographs, documents, sound and maps from one real event, laid out on one shared clock so they check each other. | Reconstructions, fact-checks, incident reports, "what really happened" explainers, disputed calls in sport, product-failure post-mortems. |

## Sketch & board

|---|---|---|---|

| Marker on Paper | [`marker-paper`](03-sketch-board/marker-paper/STYLE.md) | A worked problem in three felt-tip markers on plain white paper: black draws the situation, blue marks what we know, red marks what we want, and the same colours carry into the algebra until the red answer is circled; the camera follows the nib, pushes through a mark into the next sheet, or peels the sheet away. | Worked problems, physics and maths explainers, estimates and "how big/far/fast" questions, lessons and revision, engineering back-of-the-envelope checks. |
| One-line Drawing | [`one-line`](03-sketch-board/one-line/STYLE.md) | A single ink line that never leaves the paper. | Brand and founder stories, life or company milestones, minimal product ads, "how X became Y" explainers, tributes, a process from start to finish. |
| Sketchnote | [`sketchnote`](03-sketch-board/sketchnote/STYLE.md) | A page of lecture notes on dot-grid paper that writes itself: black fineliner, grey marker for volume and one yellow marker for what matters, while the camera rides close behind the pen and finally pulls back to show the finished page as a poster. | Lecture and course notes, science and how-it-works explainers, book and talk summaries, one-page recaps of a process, onboarding guides. |
| Stick-figure Explainer | [`stick-explainer`](03-sketch-board/stick-explainer/STYLE.md) | Clean, even-weight stick figures on warm off-white paper act out an idea about people (a chain, a crowd, a queue, a ring) while one red signal runs through them; bold sans headlines and small counters sit beside them like an editorial page, and the camera pulls back or dives in to change scale. | Social and network ideas, crowd behaviour and queues, simple statistics and probability, economics of many people (rushes, markets, voting), cause and effect in systems. |
| Tablet Sketch Explainer | [`ipad-sketch`](03-sketch-board/ipad-sketch/STYLE.md) | A drawing app's page on warm paper: a stylus pencils clean outlines over faint construction lines, then flat colour blocks are scribbled in slightly off-register, and things change by being recoloured one colour at a time while the canvas is panned, pinched and turned like a tablet in someone's hands. | Food and drink explainers, craft and how-to, how something is made, lifestyle science, before-and-after transformations, product origin stories. |
| Whiteboard Explainer | [`whiteboard`](03-sketch-board/whiteboard/STYLE.md) | An explainer drawn live on one glossy dry-erase board: single-line handwriting that is actually *written* stroke by stroke, floating markers with no hand, magnets that can move, an eraser that can rewind, and a camera that travels across the board instead of cutting. | Science and "how does X work" explainers, online course lessons, product mechanics, money maths, a comparison drawn as a duet. |
